Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the title have a 60-character guideline?

Search engines typically truncate titles beyond about 60 characters in search results, so staying under that keeps your title fully visible.

Do I need both Open Graph and Twitter Card tags?

Twitter/X falls back to Open Graph tags if Twitter Card tags are missing, but including both gives you more precise control over each platform's preview.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The meta tags are generated entirely client-side in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does CSS clamp() do?

clamp(min, preferred, max) picks the preferred value but never goes below min or above max, giving fluid scaling with safe limits.

Should I use px or rem?

rem respects the user's browser font-size setting and is generally recommended for typography; px is simpler for fixed spacing.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The clamp() value is calculated entirely client-side in your browser.

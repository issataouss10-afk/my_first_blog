Frequently Asked Questions

Why didn't it detect the QR code in my image?

Try a clearer, higher-resolution image with good contrast and the QR code not cropped or heavily angled — blurry or very small codes can be hard to decode.

Does this need my camera?

No. This tool decodes QR codes from an uploaded image file, not a live camera feed.

Is my image sent to a server?

No. Decoding happens entirely in your browser using a client-side library — your image is never uploaded.

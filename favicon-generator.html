Frequently Asked Questions

Does this generate a .ico file?

This tool generates PNG files at each standard size, which modern browsers and devices accept directly — no .ico conversion needed for most use cases.

What image works best?

A square image with a simple, bold design scales down most clearly at the smaller 16px and 32px sizes.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. Every size is rendered locally using the Canvas API — your image never leaves your device.

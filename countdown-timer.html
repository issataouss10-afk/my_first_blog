Frequently Asked Questions

Will the alert sound if my tab is in the background?

Most browsers still play the alert sound for background tabs, but behavior can vary — keep the tab open for the most reliable alert.

Does the timer keep running if I close the tab?

No, the timer only runs while the page is open in your browser.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The timer runs entirely in your browser tab.

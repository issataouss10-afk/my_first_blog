Frequently Asked Questions

Does this support milliseconds as well as seconds?

Yes. If you paste a 13-digit number, it's automatically read as milliseconds; a 10-digit number is read as seconds.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All conversions run entirely client-side in your browser for privacy and speed.

What is a Unix timestamp?

It's the number of seconds that have elapsed since January 1, 1970 (UTC), commonly called the Unix epoch.

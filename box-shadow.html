Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between blur and spread?

Blur softens the shadow's edges; spread grows or shrinks the shadow's size before blurring is applied.

Can I generate an inset (inner) shadow?

Yes. Check the "Inset shadow" box and the preview and generated code switch to an inset shadow.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The preview and CSS are generated entirely client-side in your browser.

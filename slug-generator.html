Frequently Asked Questions

Why use hyphens instead of underscores?

Search engines like Google treat hyphens as word separators but generally don't split words on underscores, making hyphens the recommended choice for SEO-friendly URLs.

What happens to accented or special characters?

Accented letters are converted to their closest plain equivalent, and other special characters and punctuation are removed to keep the slug URL-safe.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The slug is generated entirely client-side in your browser.

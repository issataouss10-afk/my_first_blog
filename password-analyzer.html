Frequently Asked Questions

Why do the crack times vary so much between rows?

Different attacks have very different guessing speeds — a rate-limited online login is far slower than an offline attack against a leaked, weakly-hashed password database.

What does the composition bar tell me?

It shows the proportion of your password made up of each character type, helping you spot if it leans too heavily on just one type, like all lowercase letters.

Is my password sent to a server?

No. Every visualization and calculation happens entirely in your browser tab.

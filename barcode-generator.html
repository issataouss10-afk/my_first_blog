Frequently Asked Questions

Which format should I use?

CODE128 is the most flexible and works with any text or numbers; EAN-13 and UPC-A are retail standards requiring an exact digit count.

Why does EAN-13 need exactly 13 digits?

EAN-13 barcodes encode 12 data digits plus a check digit, so entering a different length will show an error.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. Barcodes are generated entirely client-side in your browser.

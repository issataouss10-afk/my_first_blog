Frequently Asked Questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared (kg/m²), using standard unit conversions for imperial input.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a general population screening tool and can be less accurate for athletes, older adults, and others with different body compositions — it doesn't replace professional medical assessment.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.

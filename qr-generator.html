About Free Online QR Code Generator

Welcome to ToolNest's QR Code Generator, a fast, reliable, and completely free online utility designed to help businesses, creators, and developers convert URLs, plain text, and contact details into scannable matrix barcodes instantly.

Why Use Our QR Code Tool?

  • 100% Free: No hidden fees, subscriptions, or watermarks on generated codes.
  • Instant Generation: Built entirely client-side using high-speed JavaScript for real-time rendering.
  • Secure & Private: Your data stays in your browser; nothing is stored on external servers.
  • High-Resolution Downloads: Export clean PNG images suitable for both digital screens and print materials.

How to Create a QR Code

  1. Type or paste your destination link (URL) or message into the text box above.
  2. Click the Generate QR Code button.
  3. Preview your graphic code instantly and click Download PNG to save it to your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the error correction level do?

Higher levels let the QR code still scan correctly even if part of it is damaged or obscured, at the cost of a denser pattern.

Can I use this for Wi-Fi or contact info?

Yes. Paste a properly formatted Wi-Fi or vCard string into the text field and it will encode just like a normal URL.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The QR code is generated entirely in your browser using a client-side library.

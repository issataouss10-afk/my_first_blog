How to Use ToolNest Age Calculator

  1. Pick a birth date: Select the date of birth to calculate from.
  2. Set the "as of" date: Defaults to today, but can be any date.
  3. Read the result: See the exact age in years, months, and days.
  4. Check the stats: Total months, weeks, days, and hours lived, plus the next birthday countdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the age change depending on the "as of" date?

The calculator finds the exact age at whatever date you set, so it can be used to check age on a past or future date, not just today.

Does this account for leap years?

Yes, the calculation is based on real calendar dates, so leap years and varying month lengths are handled automatically.

Is my date of birth sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.

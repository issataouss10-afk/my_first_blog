Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my drive show less space than advertised?

Manufacturers use decimal units (1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes) while operating systems often display binary units (1 GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes), so the same drive shows a smaller number in your OS.

What's the difference between KB and KiB?

KB (kilobyte) technically means 1000 bytes in the decimal system, while KiB (kibibyte) means 1024 bytes in the binary system — though KB is often used loosely for both.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculations happen entirely client-side in your browser.

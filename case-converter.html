Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between camelCase and PascalCase?

camelCase starts with a lowercase letter (helloWorld), while PascalCase capitalizes every word including the first (HelloWorld).

What is snake_case used for?

It's common for variable and file names in languages like Python, and for database column names.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. All case conversion runs entirely client-side in your browser.

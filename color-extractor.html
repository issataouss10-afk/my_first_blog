Frequently Asked Questions

How are the dominant colors chosen?

The image's pixels are sampled and grouped by similarity, then the most common color in each group is picked to represent it.

Does image size affect the result?

Very large images are downsampled internally before analysis to keep extraction fast, without noticeably changing the resulting palette.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The entire extraction happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API — your image never leaves your device.

How to Use ToolNest Text to Speech

  1. Paste your text: Add whatever you want read aloud.
  2. Pick a voice: Choose from the voices available on your device.
  3. Adjust speed and pitch: Fine-tune how it sounds.
  4. Play it: Click Play to listen, or Stop to cancel playback.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the voice list different on my device?

Available voices come from your operating system and browser, so the list varies between devices — more voices are often available on desktop than mobile.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Speech is generated entirely on your device using the browser's built-in Web Speech API.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is "X is what % of Y" calculated?

It divides X by Y and multiplies by 100, showing what percentage X represents of the total Y.

What does a negative result mean in the change mode?

A negative percentage means the value decreased from the "from" number to the "to" number; a positive result means it increased.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "entropy" mean here?

Entropy estimates how unpredictable a password is in bits, based on its length and the variety of character types used — higher is stronger.

How is the crack time estimated?

It's a rough offline brute-force estimate assuming a high-speed guessing attack, meant as a general indicator rather than an exact figure.

Is my password sent to a server?

No. Every calculation happens in your browser tab, and nothing is logged, stored, or transmitted.

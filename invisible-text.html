Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some apps reject a truly empty message?

Many platforms block empty text fields, so people use characters that render as blank but aren't technically empty to work around that.

Why are there several different invisible characters?

Different apps filter or trim different Unicode characters, so having several options increases the chance one works where you need it.

Is my clipboard data sent to a server?

No. Copying happens entirely through your browser's clipboard API, locally.

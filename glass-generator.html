Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the effect need a background behind it?

Glassmorphism relies on backdrop-filter blurring whatever sits behind the element, so it needs a background, image, or gradient underneath to look right.

Does backdrop-filter work in every browser?

It's supported in all modern browsers. The generated CSS includes the -webkit- prefix for older Safari versions.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The preview and CSS are generated entirely client-side in your browser.

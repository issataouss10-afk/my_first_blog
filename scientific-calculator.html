How to Use ToolNest Scientific Calculator

  1. Enter your expression: Use the number and operator keys, or functions like sin, log, and √.
  2. Switch angle mode: Toggle DEG or RAD depending on how you want trig functions evaluated.
  3. Use memory: Store a value with M+, subtract with M−, and recall it anytime with MR.
  4. Get the result: Press = to evaluate, and click any history entry to reload it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this scientific calculator free?

Does it support trigonometric functions?

Yes. Sin, cos, and tan are supported and can be evaluated in either degrees or radians using the DEG/RAD toggle.

No. Every calculation runs entirely client-side in your browser.

How to Use ToolNest Image Resizer

  1. Upload an image: Click the dropzone or drag and drop a file.
  2. Set dimensions: Enter width and/or height — the lock keeps the ratio proportional.

  4. Resize and download: Click Resize Image, then Download to save the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will resizing reduce image quality?

Shrinking an image generally preserves visual quality well; enlarging it beyond its original size can make it look softer or blurry since no new detail is created.

What's the difference between the format options?

PNG is lossless and good for graphics with transparency; JPEG and WebP are compressed and better suited for photos, with an adjustable quality trade-off.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The entire resize happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API — your image never leaves your device.

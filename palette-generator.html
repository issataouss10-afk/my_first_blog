Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between these harmony types?

Complementary uses opposite hues for contrast, analogous uses neighboring hues for harmony, triadic uses three evenly spaced hues, and monochrome varies lightness of a single hue.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The palette is generated entirely client-side in your browser.

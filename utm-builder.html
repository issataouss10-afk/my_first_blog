Frequently Asked Questions

What are UTM parameters used for?

They're tags added to a URL that let analytics tools like Google Analytics attribute traffic to the specific campaign, source, and medium that sent it.

Which fields are actually required?

By convention, source, medium, and campaign name are the core three; term and content are optional and mainly useful for paid ads and split tests.

Does the saved history persist after I close the tab?

No. History is kept only in memory for this session and clears when you reload or leave the page — nothing is stored on a server.

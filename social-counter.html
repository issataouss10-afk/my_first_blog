Frequently Asked Questions

Are these limits always exactly accurate?

They reflect each platform's commonly published limits, but platforms occasionally adjust them, and some count links, mentions, or emoji differently.

Does this count emoji correctly?

Counting uses standard character length, which is a close approximation — some emoji made of multiple code points may count slightly differently than on the platform itself.

Is my post text sent to a server?

No. All counting happens entirely client-side in your browser for privacy and speed.

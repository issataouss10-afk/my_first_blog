Frequently Asked Questions

Is the tip calculated before or after tax?

This calculator applies the tip percentage directly to the bill amount you enter — enter a pre-tax or post-tax amount depending on your local convention.

Can I use decimal amounts for the bill?

Yes, the bill field accepts decimal values like 42.75 for exact totals.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. All calculation happens entirely client-side in your browser.

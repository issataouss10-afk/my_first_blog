Frequently Asked Questions

How is reading time calculated?

It's estimated at roughly 200 words per minute, a common average adult silent-reading speed.

How are sentences and paragraphs counted?

Sentences are counted by sentence-ending punctuation (. ! ?), and paragraphs by line breaks between blocks of text.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. All counting happens entirely client-side in your browser for privacy and speed.
